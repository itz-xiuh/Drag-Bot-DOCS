# Last Updated: October 24, 2026

Drag Bot ("the Bot", "we", "us", or "our") is a Discord application designed to provide voice channel management functionality, enabling authorized users to move members between voice channels efficiently.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Drag Bot may process, how that information is used, and the measures taken to protect it when the Bot is used within Discord servers.

By inviting or using Drag Bot, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

# 1. Information We Collect

Drag Bot may process certain information when necessary to provide, maintain, and secure its functionality. The information processed depends on how the Bot is configured and used.

Discord Information

The Bot may process the following Discord-related information:

- Discord User ID
- Discord Server ID
- Discord Voice Channel ID
- Relevant voice state information
- Server configuration and settings
- Permission and command interaction information

Command and Interaction Data

When users interact with Drag Bot, the Bot may process command inputs, button interactions, and user selections required to execute requested operations.

Voice Channel Information

Drag Bot may process voice state updates to identify members' current voice channels and determine whether a requested channel movement can be performed.

Drag Bot's intended functionality does not require recording or storing voice conversations.

# 2. How We Use Information

Information processed by Drag Bot may be used for the following purposes:

- Execute authorized voice channel movement requests.
- Process supported commands and interactions.
- Verify permissions before performing actions.
- Maintain server-specific configurations and preferences.
- Troubleshoot technical errors and maintain service reliability.
- Detect and prevent abuse, misuse, or unauthorized activity.

We aim to limit information processing to what is reasonably necessary to operate and maintain Drag Bot.

# 3. Voice Channel Management

Drag Bot provides tools for managing members' presence in Discord voice channels.

Authorized users may use supported commands or interactions to move members between eligible voice channels, subject to Discord's permission system and the Bot's granted permissions.

Server administrators are responsible for configuring the Bot appropriately and ensuring that its use complies with their server rules and Discord policies.

Drag Bot does not need access to the contents of voice conversations to perform its intended voice channel management functions.

# 4. Data Storage and Retention

Drag Bot may store limited configuration information when necessary for its operation, including:

- Server-specific settings
- Configured voice channels
- Feature preferences, where applicable
- Command configuration

Temporary information may be processed while a command or voice channel operation is being completed.

Information is retained only as necessary for its intended purpose, subject to applicable legal requirements. Actual storage and retention practices depend on the Bot's implementation.

# 5. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by Discord or the Bot's hosting and infrastructure providers when necessary to deliver, maintain, and secure the service.

Third-party services operate under their own terms and privacy policies. Drag Bot does not control the independent data practices of these services.

# 6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information under our control against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or misuse.

However, no electronic storage system or internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

# 7. User Data Requests

Users and authorized server administrators may contact the Drag Bot development team with questions regarding information processed or stored by the Bot.

Where applicable, requests for access to or deletion of stored information may be submitted through the official Drag Bot support server.

Requests may require verification to prevent unauthorized disclosure or deletion.

# 8. Children's Privacy

Drag Bot is intended to be used in accordance with Discord's applicable age requirements and policies.

We do not intentionally seek to collect personal information beyond what is necessary to provide the Bot's functionality.
